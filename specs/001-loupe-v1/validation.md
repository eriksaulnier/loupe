## Departures from the planning documents

Each is recorded where it applies.

- `go.mod` declares `go 1.25.0`, not `go 1.23`; `golang.org/x/term` is held at v0.45.0 (plan.md Complexity Tracking).
- The review body and each inline comment are limited to 65,536 characters instead of a 256 KiB body (plan.md Complexity Tracking).
- The `lock` refusal says to wait for or stop the holder instead of removing the lock file (contracts/cli.md).
- Labels are restricted to letters, digits, `_`, `.` and `-`, at most 40 characters (contracts/cli.md, data-model.md).
- `list --json` returns its rows under `runs` (contracts/cli.md).
- Branch resolution reads `branch.<name>.merge` and `branch.<name>.remote` before falling back to the origin owner (plan.md Design Notes).
- `--retry-unknown` reconciles again under the retaken lock before overwriting the attempt (plan.md Design Notes).

- What the automatic triggers do to each other (specs/007-unattended-publish FR-028). FR-028 wanted a live round judged before each further trigger; `opened` and `ready_for_review` shipped in `667e6b9` before that happened, so the first rounds under them are also the first evidence about them. On 2026-09-17 `#12` and `#13` were each opened ready and then labelled within eight seconds, firing an automatic round and a labelled one on the same commit. The concurrency group held both times: on `#13` the labelled round sat queued from 18:07:14 and ran no step before it was cancelled at 18:08:00, and on `#12` the labelled round waited from 18:06:35 until 18:08:08, four seconds after the automatic round was cancelled. The automatic round's `unlabel` job is skipped, so the label stayed on in both. What neither pull request showed is the duplicate: a round was cancelled in both cases before a second review could land, and nothing records what cancelled it, so a labelled round has never been left to finish behind an automatic one that published. That it would publish a second review on the same commit is read off the eligibility step, where a label bypasses the already-reviewed gate, and is unfixed in `eriksaulnier/loupe-workflows`.
